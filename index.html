Features

Everything you need, nothing screaming for attention.

Icon rail

Nine hand-drawn icons in one quiet gold pill. Hover for fade-in labels, click for a card — one at a time, never stacking. Every window drags; your layout persists.

Shop glow

Pin a comp and its units pulse with a gold ring directly on your shop cards — the Team-Planner glow, for any comp you choose.

Plan card

Your pinned comp as a living checklist: early board, late board, one-tap pivots — and recommended items per unit from real pick-rate data.

Choice & augment assist

At augment and God picks, cards appear under the offers: your tier notes, and a gold "Fits your comp" when a choice matches your plan.

Post-game coach

Placement and econ grades, highlights, augment tendencies — refreshed automatically when your match ends. Plus a lobby board for scouting by name.

Item dock

All ten components, every recipe, all emblems. Tap one for its builds, two for the exact craft, hover for details — verified against live set data.

The Trainer

Aim Lab for rolldowns.

Set a level, a target unit and a gold budget — then roll a simulated shop with the real odds and the real bag sizes. When you stop, CompMaster grades your rolldown against the median and 80% expected cost. Ten drills between queues and your fingers stop hesitating at 4-1.

No other TFT tool has this. It runs fully offline — no game client needed.

Compliance

Designed around Riot's third-party policy — not against it.

Static · always on

Odds tables, bag sizes, comps, items, positioning maps, the Trainer, the post-game coach. Reference material and player-chosen plans — explicitly fine to show all game.

Contextual · market practice

Shop glow on your pinned comp, augment tier notes at offer time, purchase-inferred board tracking. The same class of assist shipped by registered tools today; scope to be confirmed at our Riot registration.

Full assist · gated

Live pool odds, scored buy/sell advice, board-scanned recommendations. Built and tested in the Trainer — shipped in-game only if and when Riot approves it. Off by default, no exceptions.

Hard rules: no memory reading · no code injection · no input automation · screen reading only · ads never appear in-game (Riot policy, May 2025) · Riot third-party product registration: application in progress.

FAQ

Straight answers.
Is this allowed by Riot?

CompMaster is built to sit inside Riot's third-party policy: everything shown in-game is either static reference or player-chosen plans surfaced contextually, matching what registered tools ship today. Our product registration with Riot is in progress; the full live-assist tier stays disabled until it's approved.

Can using it get me banned?

Honest answer: until registration completes, any unknown unsigned overlay carries non-zero risk with automated anti-cheat — that's true of every indie tool at this stage. CompMaster never reads memory, injects code, or automates input, and we recommend testing against VODs or the mobile-mirror workflow rather than ranked play until our registration is confirmed. We will always tell you exactly where this stands.

Does it read the game's memory?

No. CompMaster only reads pixels from your screen — the same technique OBS uses — plus Riot's public web API for post-game data, with your own key.

How does it stay current each patch?

Set data (units, items, emblems, augment names) loads live from Community Dragon at every start. Comps, augment notes and item stats are editable files — updated each patch, with a one-command crawler for real pick-rate percentages.
